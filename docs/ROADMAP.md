# Choudhary & Sons Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Company roles: owner, admin, HR, project manager, accountant, site supervisor, employee and applicant
- Authentication and permissions
- Employee profiles
- Admin dashboard shell
- Employee mobile dashboard shell
- PostgreSQL-ready backend

## Phase 2 — HR, Attendance and Payroll
- Employee onboarding and document records
- Attendance check-in/check-out
- Shift and overtime rules
- Leave requests and approvals
- Monthly payroll
- Salary advances, deductions and bonuses
- Downloadable salary slips

## Phase 3 — Contracts and Projects
- Clients and departments
- Tender/opportunity pipeline
- Contract value, dates and status
- Project sites and responsible managers
- BOQ items and quantities
- Daily site progress reports with photos
- Work orders and assigned tasks
- Variation orders and extension tracking
- Project document register

## Phase 4 — Procurement and Supply
- Supplier directory and ratings
- Material catalog
- Purchase requisitions
- Approval workflow
- Requests for quotation
- Purchase orders
- Goods receipt and site stock
- Material issue/transfer records
- Low-stock alerts

## Phase 5 — Finance and Commercial Controls
- Project expenses
- Vendor bills and payments
- Client invoices and receivables
- Retention and security deposits
- Petty cash
- Project budget vs actual
- Profitability and cash-flow reporting

## Phase 6 — Assets and Compliance
- Machinery and vehicle register
- Fuel logs
- Maintenance schedules
- Equipment assignment
- Safety inspections and incidents
- Employee certifications and expiry reminders
- Insurance/license/document expiry alerts

## Phase 7 — Recruitment and Employee Self-Service
- Public jobs board
- Online applications and CV records
- Shortlisting/interview status
- Offer and hiring workflow
- Employee salary, attendance and leave view
- Expense claims
- Company announcements
- Assigned project/tasks

## Phase 8 — Management Intelligence
- Owner dashboard
- Contract pipeline value
- Project completion and delay indicators
- Payroll summaries
- Outstanding client payments
- Supplier liabilities
- Project profit margins
- Attendance trends
- Audit logs and exportable reports

## High-value contractor features
- BOQ-based cost control
- Daily site diary with progress photos
- Tender expiry/deadline reminders
- Supplier comparison and quotation history
- Approval chain for purchases and expenses
- Geo-tag-ready attendance/site reporting
- Contract and document expiry alerts
- Project-wise manpower and payroll cost
- Equipment utilization and maintenance reminders
- Complete audit trail for sensitive changes
