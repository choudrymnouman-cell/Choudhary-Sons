# Choudhary & Sons Management Platform

A complete operations platform for a civil contractor and supplier business.

## Product scope

The system is designed around two main experiences:

- **Admin / Management:** contracts, projects, employees, attendance, payroll, procurement, inventory, suppliers, finance, machinery, documents, safety, approvals and reports.
- **Employee / Applicant:** profile, attendance, salary slips, assigned work, leave, expenses, documents, announcements and job applications.

## Planned modules

1. Authentication and role-based access
2. Company dashboard
3. Employees and HR records
4. Attendance and shifts
5. Payroll and salary slips
6. Recruitment and job applications
7. Contracts, tenders and clients
8. Projects, sites and daily progress
9. BOQ and cost tracking
10. Suppliers and purchase orders
11. Materials, stock and site inventory
12. Expenses, payments and project profitability
13. Machinery, vehicles and maintenance
14. Safety incidents and compliance
15. Leave, approvals and notifications
16. Document storage and expiry reminders
17. Reports and analytics
18. Audit log and activity history

## Technology

- Flutter for the mobile application
- FastAPI for the backend API
- PostgreSQL for production data
- SQLAlchemy for persistence
- JWT-based authentication
